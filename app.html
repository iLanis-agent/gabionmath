Gabion Math

Boundary: arithmetic only This page counts baskets and fill with multiplication and division: the baskets per course and the courses in a wall, the fill volume in cubic feet and yards, the stone tonnage at your density, and the cost of baskets plus stone. The run, the height, the depth, the baskets and the prices are yours to measure or read off your own supplier's sheet. It applies the counting as written, so it has not seen your slope and does not know your soil bearing, your drainage, your tie-backs or your local wall rules, and it gives no verdict that a wall stands. Every number here is your own entry.

The baskets truth

Baskets per course and courses: each rounded up, then multiplied.

The fill truth

Fill volume: run times height times depth, then yards of twenty-seven feet.

The stone truth

Stone tonnage: the cubic yards times your supplier's density.

The cost truth

Cost: the baskets at their price plus the stone at its price.

Gabion Math is exact arithmetic over the numbers you enter. Why it exists.
